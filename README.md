## Project Overview
Bayan is a mobile application that provides a personal digital medical portfolio, bringing each person’s medical history together in one accessible place. It solves the problem of scattered medical records by organizing diagnoses, treatments, laboratory results, and medical imaging. Designed for all ages, it allows parents to manage their children’s profiles from birth. Users can share selected records with doctors, access AI-assisted summaries, and view essential information during emergencies. Its long-term vision is to become a unified digital health identity across public hospitals, private hospitals, and independent medical practices through official collaboration with the Ministry of Health. The project will be presented as a high-fidelity interactive prototype demonstrating the main user journeys.

---

## Project Objectives
- Centralize medical records in an organized portfolio accessible to users and authorized doctors.
- Support continuity of care through controlled record sharing, specialty-focused views, and doctor consultation options.
- Provide quick access to essential emergency information, emergency contacts, and an ambulance calling feature.
- Support health management through AI assistance, medication tracking, and family profile management.

---

## Project Scope
- **Medical profiles and history:** Personal and child profiles containing medical timelines, diagnoses, treatments, laboratory results, medical imaging, allergies, and existing conditions.
- **Emergency access:** A dedicated screen displaying essential health information and emergency contacts, with QR code or card access and an ambulance calling option.
- **Privacy and record sharing:** Biometric sign-in on supported devices, permission-based record sharing, and caregiver access management.
- **Doctor interaction:** Doctor consultation flows and customizable views that prioritize information relevant to the doctor’s specialty.
- **Health assistance:** AI-assisted medical history summaries and explanations, medication reminders, and dose tracking.

> **Final Deliverable:** A high-fidelity interactive mobile application prototype demonstrating medical profile management, medical history browsing, record sharing, doctor consultation, AI assistance, medication tracking, and emergency access. Features will use simulated interactions. Live services, direct ambulance dispatch, and official Ministry of Health integration are outside the five-week prototype scope.

## Project Plan (5 Weeks)

### Week 1 — UX Research & Planning
- Conduct UX research, user interviews, and competitor analysis; define user needs, explore solutions, prioritize features, and develop user flows.

### Week 2 — Low-Fidelity & Mid-Fidelity Wireframes
- Create and refine low-fidelity and mid-fidelity wireframes for the main screens and user journeys.

### Week 3 — Design System & High-Fidelity Design
- Build the design system, including colors, typography, and reusable components, and begin high-fidelity screen designs.

### Week 4 — High-Fidelity Design & Prototyping
- Complete the high-fidelity screens, review visual consistency, and connect the screens into an interactive prototype.

### Week 5 — Usability Testing & Refinement
- Test key use cases with representative users, document findings, refine the prototype, and prepare the final presentation.
